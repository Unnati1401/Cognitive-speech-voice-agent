# DementiaBank access request (independent researcher)

**Process:** Register on TalkBank (green *Login/Register* button, top-right of https://talkbank.org),
read the DementiaBank *Ground Rules*, then send the email below to **talkbank@cmu.edu**.

**Independent-researcher note:** the consortium is used to academic sign-ups, and students
normally need a faculty sponsor. As an independent researcher, be explicit about (a) your
research purpose, (b) that this is non-commercial and clinician-in-the-loop, and (c) that you
will follow the Ground Rules (no redistribution, no re-identification). If they require an
institutional affiliation, fall back to the open PROCESS/PROCESS-2 corpus (see README) and
mention you are happy to revisit once affiliated.

---

**To:** talkbank@cmu.edu
**Subject:** DementiaBank consortium access request - independent researcher (ADReSS / Pitt corpus)

Dear TalkBank / DementiaBank team,

I would like to request access to the DementiaBank consortium data, in particular the Pitt
corpus and the ADReSS subset, for a non-commercial research project.

**About me:** I am an independent machine-learning researcher (Unnati, unnati@linkcapital.net).
I am not currently affiliated with a degree-granting institution; I am conducting this work as
an independent research and portfolio project.

**Intended use:** I am developing an interpretable, speech-based *screening* method that
extracts cognitive-linguistic markers (pause patterns, lexical diversity, syntactic complexity,
discourse coherence) from short picture-description samples, and evaluates how well a
transparent model distinguishes controls from participants with a dementia diagnosis. The aim
is methodological and educational: an explainable, clinician-in-the-loop screening aid, framed
explicitly as a research demonstration and **not** a diagnostic or medical device. No clinical
deployment and no commercial product are involved.

**Data handling:** I will keep all data on a single local machine, will not redistribute or
re-share any recordings or transcripts, will make no attempt to re-identify participants, and
will cite DementiaBank per the required references. I have read and agree to the DementiaBank
Ground Rules.

If independent access is not possible without an institutional affiliation, I would be grateful
for any guidance on the appropriate path, and will otherwise proceed with openly available
challenge corpora in the meantime.

Thank you for maintaining this resource.

Best regards,
Unnati
unnati@linkcapital.net
