# Cognitive Speech Screening — research demo

Upload, record, or click a sample picture-description clip. The app transcribes it,
extracts cognitive-linguistic markers (pauses, vocabulary diversity, syntactic
complexity, discourse coherence), scores them, and drafts a grounded screening report.

**Research demonstration only. Not a diagnosis and not a medical device.** Scores in
this hosted demo come from a placeholder model trained on synthetic clips; they are for
showing the pipeline, not for any clinical interpretation.
